Enemies

Sep 28, 20266 min read

  • Keep in mind that throwing weapons have a really strong AGI modifier. If an enemy has a ludicrous AGI requirement to first-turn-kill, they may still be killable with a throwing weapon.
    • Rock: +12 AGI.
      • Note: Single-target. Medium/high damage.
    • Bwaka Knife: +25 AGI.
      • Note: AOE, but hits everything. Does basically no damage, but bleeds and stuns.
    • Throwing Knives: +25 AGI.
      • Note: Single-target. High damage.
    • Shuriken: +25 AGI.
      • Note: Single-target. Very high damage.
    • Fan Daggers: +50 AGI.
      • Note: AOE, so might not hit the desired limb(s).
      • Low damage.
    • Throwing Daggers +50 AGI.
      • Note: AOE, so might not hit the desired limb(s).
      • Medium damage.
  • Remember that enemies have a defense stat. If it’s high enough, it may screw you out of a decapitation.
