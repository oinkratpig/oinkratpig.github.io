Death Penalty

Sep 30, 20262 min read

  • Guide 1
  • Guide 2
  • Destroying bodies will add negative status effects unless wearing a gas mask.
  • Destroying bodies does not pause the game. Enemies still move around.
    • Be very careful destroying bodies if enemies are nearby because they can ambush you mid-animation.
  • Getting to courtyard:
    1. Go to library.
    2. Use the catgirl painting.
    3. Directions:
      1. Right
      2. Up
      3. Left
      4. Up
      5. Left
      6. Go through the door the Freak is guarding
      7. All the way left to pick up the courtyard key on the floor next to the Devil
    4. Retrace steps
    5. In the library, go down the broken bars to get to the first hub OR just walk there
    6. Go to the top-left of the first hub to get to the mold rooms
    7. Go to the very top-right of the mold rooms to enter the courtyard
  • Attack elements of fire and holy both do damage to ghostly enemies.

Combat

  • If you can’t break an enemy’s weapon arm in one hit, go for legs.
  • Throwing weapons are the GOAT.
